Training notes & safety reminders
  • Strength sessions: start with two sets; work toward more only if recovery is good.
  • Use a stable low stair and handrail for step-ups. Step down slowly.
  • Choose a band tension that allows smooth, controlled repetitions. Leave 2–3 good reps in reserve.
  • Walking is welcome daily. Cycling can be easy and weather-permitting; ease off if your legs need recovery.
  • No jumping. Stop or modify any movement that causes back pain. Keep the range comfortable.
  • Yoga and Pilates are separate lighter sessions on their scheduled days.
  • Crawling + core day: bear, crab and army crawls (one 24-foot pass each), bird dogs (2 × 10 per side), dead bugs (2 × 10 total), and pushback push-up variation (1 × 10). Progress only while movement stays controlled and pain-free.
  • Back felt great during the first crawling + core session. Mild leg soreness after leg day is okay if it improves with warm-up and does not change your movement.
