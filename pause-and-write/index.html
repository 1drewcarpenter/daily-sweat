Before you eatSaved on this device

Take a moment to check in

If you feel pulled toward extra food, pause here first if you can. Take a breath. You don't have to solve everything right now.

A gentle reminder
Hunger is not a failure, and eating is not something to feel ashamed of. This exercise is simply a way to understand what you need in this moment.
1

What am I feeling right now?

Describe what's happening in your body, mind, and emotions. Try to be specific and honest.

2

What do I think the extra food will do for me?

Comfort me? Distract me? Help me relax? Fill some kind of emptiness? Something else?

3

What is the likely result if I eat it?

Look beyond the first few minutes. What might happen physically and emotionally?

4

How do I think I will feel an hour later?

Imagine yourself one hour from now. What might you be thinking or feeling?

5

What might I actually need right now?

Is there another way to respond to the feeling? A call, a little rest, a change of scene, a meal if you're hungry, or simply being kind to yourself?

6

What support can I reach for?

Could I contact another OA member or someone I trust? If making the call feels difficult, write what you would want to say.

After the moment — optional follow-up

If you'd like, come back later and record what happened. Whatever happened, this is information, not a grade.

Remember

You do not have to make a perfect choice. You can pause, reach out, eat if you're hungry, and begin again at any time.
